# Stage B event data: sources, actual use and remaining gaps

The accepted Stage B study is a **hypothetical 0.66 km² engineered installation**, driven by regional event weather for **2022-09-09 00:00 through 2022-09-15 00:00 UTC**. Sources were downloaded and processed before the completed simulations. G1–G6 approved their stated uses and assumptions for the exploratory case; the current analysis fixes the gate schedule and optimizes wetland designs. The [B overview](../README.md), [accepted source/assumption guide](../data_sources_and_assumptions.md), [wetland-only results](12_wetland_only_optimization.md) and [dynamic comparison](13_no_wetland_counterfactual.md) describe the current study.

The core retrieval window is September 2–15, 2022 UTC, including antecedent data. NOAA includes one bracketing report on either side. A separate USGS September 15–18 extract checks the observed recession without extending the six-day model horizon. Observations, derived values, gridded estimates, spatial transfers, engineering assumptions and numerical settings have different evidentiary roles; a source citation does not turn an assumed parameter into a measurement.

## Missing or unsuitable evidence

- **USGS rainfall:** the [00045 query](https://waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/iv/?format=json&sites=04087088&startDT=2022-09-02T00%3A00%3A00Z&endDT=2022-09-15T00%3A00%3A00Z&parameterCd=00045&siteStatus=all) returned no time series. Unavailable rainfall is not zero rainfall.
- **KMWC rainfall:** only 42 one-hour intervals passed the relevant conditions, with gaps and overlap. The station is used for meteorology, not a complete rain series. Absent `AA` fields do not become zero.
- **KMKE hourly detail:** two independent one-hour amounts are missing on September 13, 12:52–14:52 UTC. An observed three-hour trace ending at 14:52 covers the aggregate period; the overlapping one-hour trace is excluded. Its hourly distribution and exact trace amount remain unknown.
- **MMSD:** the tested [historical rain interface](https://www.mmsd.com/about-us/milwaukee-rain-facility-information) returned all 17 gauges as 0.00 with 2026 last-timestamp fields for a 2022 request, without interval quality/completeness. The response is unsuitable and excluded. This does not establish that MMSD has no historical archive; an authoritative WS1229 historical interval export could improve spatial rainfall evidence.
- **Site measurements:** no suitable local radiation instrument, hydraulically connected shallow groundwater well, initial soil-water/storage record, blockage inspection, facility survey or quantitative vegetation-response record was obtained. Those quantities must not be presented as locally measured.
- **Identified but unused datasets:** NLDAS was not downloaded through an authenticated service. LiDAR, SSURGO and Wisconsin Wetland Inventory metadata do not supply the assumed facility levels, bathymetry, synthetic vegetation properties or lumped soil coefficients. No values were extracted from them for this simulation.

## Geographic mapping and datum

Structured USGS station metadata give **18.1 mi² = 46.8787847970816 km²**, rounded to 46.879 km², and NAD83 coordinates **43.0547222, −88.0461111**. The legacy narrative instead gives latitude 43°03′00″, while the structured record gives 43°03′17″. That discrepancy remains unresolved; the context point is not a surveyed installation outlet. The downloaded WBD HUC12 Underwood Creek polygon 040400030404 has agency attribute area **50.72 km²** and downstream HUC 040400030405. NLDI split-at-station requests failed HTTP 502. A retrieved simplified whole-catchments polygon has approximately **50.56 km²** by a spherical diagnostic; neither polygon area substitutes for the station drainage area. Exact source requests and returned geography remain recorded in local `geography_retrieval.json`; the [source catalog](../source_catalog.json) identifies their agencies and endpoints.

The approved installation has its own analytical area budget and assumed receiving channel. The real County Grounds diversion/tunnel arrangement is not the two hypothetical dam–gate–wetland branches. No facility polygon is fabricated, and observed total gauge discharge is not inserted as `Q_ext`. Model elevations stay in an assumed common datum; observed approximately 212 m NAVD88 levels cannot be compared directly with simulated approximately 97–101 m levels. No site-specific safety threshold is derived from those gauge peaks.

The observed event peaks are **28.203579 m³/s** on September 12 at 08:45 and 08:50 UTC and **212.122512 m NAVD88** at 08:50. The paired observed elevation and gage-height series verify the **680.14 ft** 2022 datum offset. These values characterize the surrounding event, not the model's predicted or observed output. Details and precision are in the [USGS report](04_usgs_observations.md).

## Prepared forcing and completed checks

[prepare_b2_data.py](../prepare_b2_data.py) builds **4,321 finite 120 s knots** from processed observations, the grid estimate and approved constant boundaries. The source rain accumulation periods and solar hourly energies are preserved with nonnegative piecewise-linear reconstruction and 120 s boundary ramps. Dry intervals stay zero; trace flags remain separately recorded. Temperature/wind interpolate accepted bracketing observations without extrapolation. Finer integration steps reuse the same prepared knots rather than creating a different storm.

The completed **120/60/30 s** comparison runs preserve **145.300000 mm rainfall**, corresponding to **95,898 m³** over the hypothetical **660,000 m²** surface footprint, and **21166.810000 Wh/m²** shortwave energy. Rainfall volume is derived by depth × approved area; the area is assumed, so this volume is a model input calculation, not a measured site runoff volume. The prepared CSV SHA-256 is `883135c877699ec0354f7a4abdcaffe326953293f88fe1dc7d5d6d7824336394`. Actual execution checks are in the [dynamic comparison report](13_no_wetland_counterfactual.md#time-resolution-provenance-and-remaining-storage).

NOAA/USGS processing, timestamp/coverage checks, checksum verification and forcing-integral checks were completed. These are data-processing and numerical checks, not calibration or field validation. The full [data-source and assumption guide](../data_sources_and_assumptions.md) separately documents geometry, hydraulic limits, gate coefficients, vegetation endpoints and numerical settings.

## Retained provenance and access

Raw responses, exact requests/payloads, retrieval times, checksums, quality flags, rejected/overlapping rain records and processed UTC/SI files remain local under ignored `B/work/event_data/`. Local processing scripts are `B/work/fetch_usgs_event.py` and `B/work/fetch_weather_event.py`. These are not downloadable files in the public repository; the detailed source guides expose their source requests, methods and relevant hashes without creating broken repository links. No annual NOAA files or basin-wide bulk data were downloaded.

The initial weather QC report and source-interval CSV retain their preparation-time `proposal_approved=False` or `not_model_used` flags. Those unchanged source records describe their original preparation status. Current accepted use is recorded separately by `approved=true` in the prepared-forcing manifest and frozen [model configuration](../no_spillway_config.json). Approval does not change the observations or resolve limitations such as traces, spatial representativeness or unsupported site properties.

## Limited support from physical references

The [FAO56 Chapter 3 relationships](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e07.htm) support the existing vapor-pressure relation and selected constants: latent heat `2.45e6 J/kg`, air heat capacity `1013 J/(kg K)`, molecular-weight ratio `0.622` and water-density approximation `1000 kg/m³`. The JMA/UCAR constant reference cataloged in [source_catalog.json](../source_catalog.json) supports `R_v=461.5 J/(kg K)`; the cataloged NIST standard supports gravity `9.80665 m/s²`. These references do not validate the reduced ET closure. FAO wetland crop coefficients are not this model's `K_veg`, and Manning roughness is not the lumped resistance `R_w`.

DNR 1001 does not supply the proposed flood-containment levels. The Wisconsin Pond 378 reference informs a freeboard concept only; all absolute crest elevations and resulting hypothetical safety levels remain explicitly assumed, as explained in the [accepted assumptions](../data_sources_and_assumptions.md#fixed-design-limits-and-gate-applicability). Synthetic category endpoints have no claim to measured Milwaukee vegetation properties.
