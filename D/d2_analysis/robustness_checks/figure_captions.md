# Robustness-check figures

1. [Zero-peak diagnostics](plots/01_zero_peak_diagnostics.png). Panels A–B retain all 320 primary paired outcomes. Panels C–D use the 258 retained full-array pairs, including all 87 zero-primary pairs. Maximum absolute waveform difference and the separately labeled rain-response peak do not replace the full-window primary metric. Every zero-primary pair has both first maxima at time zero and different later waveforms.

2. [Event hydrographs](plots/02_event_hydrographs.png). Examples use the lowest saved sample ID with a zero primary difference and the lowest ID with a positive difference in each event. Columns can have different parameter values; they are not a single-parameter intervention. All 144 hours and the initial-stage reference remain visible. Shading marks the original largest quantified wet spell, not a new flood threshold. The benchmark has no zero pair. Stage scales match within rows; rows can differ.

3. [Initial storage and balance](plots/03_storage_balance.png). All five events have 64 paired recorded endpoints. The −5,500 m³ initial term, final system-storage contrast, non-outlet external term and recorded numerical residual recover cumulative outlet differences. These are bookkeeping terms, not a causal partition of tagged initial water. Final wetland-compartment storage uses a log scale; it differs from storage across the entire system. Boxplots display median, interquartile range and 1.5-IQR whiskers.

4. [ECDF zero atoms](plots/04_ecdf_zero_atoms.png). Exact count/N CDFs retain zero jumps for August 2019 and August 2024; the right panel compares zero counts for all five fixed events. Bands/intervals are 95% pointwise exact-binomial limits under the original exploratory parameter law. They are not simultaneous bands, total physical uncertainty or historical weather probabilities. Empirical F(0) and exact-zero mass coincide here because no negative value was sampled; their population definitions remain distinct.

5. [Sample-size stability](plots/05_sample_size_stability.png). Original-order prefixes and 1,000 matched permutation subsets use only existing vectors. Ribbons show the middle 95% of finite-set subset statistics, not population confidence intervals. The same subset IDs are used across events. At 64 every subset is the entire original set, so collapse of the ribbon is automatic and does not prove convergence.

6. [Parameter-law checks](plots/06_parameter_law_checks.png). Self-normalized reweighting compares the original log-uniform law, independent uniform multipliers on the same bounds, and ±0.25 latent infiltration/routing correlation stresses. River resistance is independent in the two dependence stresses. No new parameter vectors or physical simulations are produced. ESS measures concentration of weights; no weighted confidence bands or calibrated prior probabilities are claimed. Nonnegative weighted contrasts alone are not new validation of mitigation.
